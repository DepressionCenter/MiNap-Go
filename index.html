MiNap Go

A browser-based sleep diary. Tap Sleep, tap Wake, and answer a short morning survey -- no account needed to try it, and no extra technology to run a study.

Try the standalone app

What MiNap Go is

MiNap Go is a sleep diary built on the Consensus Sleep Diary, the instrument researchers commonly use to study sleep. Two taps record when you fall asleep and when you wake, and a short daily survey fills in the rest.

It comes in two versions that never share data with each other:

  • The standalone app keeps everything on your own device. No account, no server, and it works offline once installed.
  • The study version is what a researcher deploys for a research study. It saves each participant's entries to that researcher's own private Google Sheet.

Track your own sleep

The standalone app runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere, and you can install it so it keeps working without a connection.

For researchers

Running a study with MiNap Go takes a Google account and a copy of the template Sheet -- no server to host and no code to write. Each study gets its own Sheet, and only your enrolled participants' IDs can log data to it.
