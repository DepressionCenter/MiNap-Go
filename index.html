MiNap Go

A remarkably simple, browser-based sleep tracker. One tap when you fall asleep, and one tap when you wake up. An optional daily survey defaults to research-ready questions, giving you complete control over your sleep data.

For Individuals

The Standalone App

The standalone app keeps everything on your own device. It requires no accounts, communicates with no servers, and is packed with features designed for everyday use:

  • Universal & Offline Runs directly in any modern browser. You can install it to your home screen so it keeps working completely without a connection.
  • Wearable Ready Fully functional on smartwatches that support a web browser, making tracking from bed a breeze.
  • Absolute Privacy Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Your sensitive sleep data stays locked tightly on your local hardware.
  • Shareable Insights Easily export your data or generate a comprehensive report to share securely with your healthcare provider.

For Researchers

The Study Version

Stop worrying about deployment logistics and start collecting data today. The study version is what a researcher deploys for a research study. It completely eliminates the barriers to digital sleep research:

  • Ready-to-Run & Zero Cost This is the standalone version of the original MiNap smartwatch app. Experience research-grade tracking with zero infrastructure required and zero hosting costs.
  • Zero Code Deployment Skip the IT department. Running a study with MiNap Go takes only a Google account and a copy of the template Sheet -- no server to host and absolutely no code to write.
  • Secure Data Silos Each study gets its own independent Sheet, securely logging only enrolled participant IDs. Data is never mingled between studies.
